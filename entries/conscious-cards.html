conscious cards

96 questions, 6 emotions. a deck designed to help two people make space for the conversation they would otherwise avoid. the questions aren't icebreakers — they're the kind that, asked at the right moment, let someone become more honest with themselves.

the brief was simple to say and difficult to do: a card deck that doesn't feel like a wellness product, a self-help product, or a party game — and yet sits comfortably between all three.

the approach

each card is one question. no instructions, no scoring, no app. the packaging is the entire interface — choose a card, ask the question, listen.

six emotions organize the deck — not as categories the user picks from, but as a scaffold the writer of each question worked within. the reader never sees them; they're the structure under the surface.

outcome

the deck is now in circulation — used in homes, retreats, and one-on-one conversations. it became the anchor product for we reflect, shaping the brand around the question of how strangers (and partners) become honest with each other.

what surprised us most: people don't put it down after one card. once the first question breaks the surface, the rest pull themselves out.
