Conscious Cards

Date APR 2026 / 18:24 Medium Card deck · Brand · Product For We Reflect Role Co-design, brand
[ image placeholder · 16:9 ]

96 questions, 6 emotions. Conscious Cards is a deck designed to help two people make space for the conversation they would otherwise avoid. The questions are not icebreakers; they are the kind of question that, asked at the right moment, lets someone become more honest with themselves.

The brief was simple to say and difficult to do: a card deck that does not feel like a wellness product, a self-help product, or a party game — and yet sits comfortably between all three.

The card is not the experience. The card is the permission slip.

Approach

Each card is one question. The card has no instructions, no prompts, no scoring, no app. The packaging is the entire interface — choose a card, ask the question, listen.

Six emotions organize the deck — not as categories the user picks from, but as a scaffold the writer of each question worked within. The reader never sees them; they're the structure under the surface.

What's in the box

96 questions, printed on uncoated card stock. A small instruction card that you only need once. A box that closes with the satisfying sound of two pieces of card finding each other.
